Indian Student & Jobseeker Safety Handbook

National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930

If you have made payments or transferred money to a fraudulent recruiter via UPI/IMPS, call 1930 immediately to freeze the beneficiary account. Lodge complaints at cybercrime.gov.in.

1. Zero-Fee Indian Hiring Rule

Under Ministry of Labour & Employment directives, registered companies and recruitment partners never charge candidates for interviews, aptitude tests, security deposits, or hardware delivery. Any request for a refundable deposit is an immediate advance-fee scam.

2. Official Domain Verification

Never rely on email display names. Examine the actual domain after the '@' symbol. Scammers often use free webmail (e.g. @gmail.com) or lookalike typo domains (e.g. @tcs-careers-onboarding.in instead of @tcs.com).

3. Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA21) Check

Verify legitimate company existence on the official MCA portal (mca.gov.in). Check company incorporation date, registered office, and active status.

4. Never Share Sensitive Documents Prematurely

Do not upload Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, voter IDs, bank passbook copies, or OTPs before independently verifying the employer via official telephone numbers or campus placement officers.

How to Configure SerpApi for Live Searches:

  1. Register for a free key at serpapi.com (includes 250 free searches/month according to hackathon terms; please verify current account terms).
  2. Open the project root directory and edit your .env file: 
    SERPAPI_KEY=your_actual_serpapi_key_here
  3. Restart the Flask backend application: 
    python app.py
  4. OpportunityShield AI will automatically detect the live key and switch to real-time queries!
Security Note: OpportunityShield AI follows strict security standards. SERPAPI_KEY is maintained strictly on the server-side environment and is never exposed to client-side scripts, network responses, or GitHub repositories.
